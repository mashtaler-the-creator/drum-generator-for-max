How the controls work
Kit
What the MIDI targets. GM / Ableton Drum Rack uses the General MIDI note map (kick C1, snare D1, hats F#1/A#1 — matches a Drum Rack built from C1 up). Vermona DRM1 MKIII re-voices every pattern onto the module's 8 channels using its factory notes on MIDI channel 10 — no MIDI-learn needed.
Pattern
The base groove, grouped by style. Entries marked (fill) are one-bar turnarounds — you can render them alone, but the engine also drops fills in automatically every few bars of any groove.
Feel preset
The humanization character. machine — grid-perfect, robotic on purpose. tight — subtle session-drummer timing and velocity drift. breaks — loose jungle/breakbeat feel: laid-back snare, pushed hats, active ghost notes.
Bars / BPM
Length and tempo of the rendered part. BPM auto-fills with the pattern's native tempo when you pick one — override it freely.
Density
Thins out the non-accent hits. 1.00 plays the full pattern; lower values keep the backbone (accents never drop) and remove decoration.
Ghosts
Probability of extra low-velocity ghost notes (snare/hat). preset keeps the feel preset's own amount; drag right for a busier, more human part.
🎲 New variation
Rolls a new random seed — a new take of the same part. The seed shown on the right identifies the take: the same seed with the same settings always reproduces it exactly.
▶ Play / loop
Audition with the built-in synthesized preview kit. It's an approximation for judging the groove — your real kit will sound better.
