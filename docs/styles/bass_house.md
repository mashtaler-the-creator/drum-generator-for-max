# Bass House (UK bass house / Night Bass)

**Tempo range:** 125–130 BPM (126–128 is the sweet spot; the speed-garage end pushes to 130).

## Drum DNA

Bass house keeps the four-on-the-floor house skeleton — 4x4 kick, big clap on beats 2 and 4, offbeat open hat — but everything on top is inherited from UK garage rather than tech house. That means real swing (58–65% MPC-style, versus tech house's near-straight 50–53%), skippy 16th-note hat lattices with strong velocity contour, rim skips, and ghost hits that make the groove limp rather than march. The offbeat open hat is the bounce anchor and stays loud; the closed hat chokes it to shape the bounce. Kicks are punchy but stay out of the bass's way, and in drops the drums go deliberately lean so the talky bass riff and clipped vocal chops lead. Claps are layered and slightly loose. The lineage runs from fidget house (Jack Beats) and Birmingham speed garage/bassline through to AC Slater's Night Bass, Chris Lorenzo, Taiki Nulight and My Nu Leng.

In this engine, swing 0 = straight and ~0.33 = full triplet, so garage-style 58–65% swing maps to roughly 0.16–0.30, and the tighter tech-leaning grooves sit at 0.08–0.14.

## The 10 approaches

1. **bh_night_bass_bounce** — The canonical Night Bass groove: 4x4 kick, backbeat clap, loud offbeat open hats, skippy closed-hat pickups, bar-2 kick pickup and clap flam. Reference: AC Slater "Bass Inside", Night Bass label sound.
2. **bh_ukg_skip** — The garage inheritance: heavy 0.24 swing, full 16th closed-hat lattice with sharp velocity contour, rim skips, shaker bed, open hats only on offbeats 2 and 4. Reference: My Nu Leng, Chris Lorenzo "Mami"-era grooves.
3. **bh_fidget_stab** — Fidget chop: nearly straight, syncopated clap stabs off the backbeat, cowbell and high-tom jabs trading across bars, space for chopped vocals. Reference: Jack Beats, early Jamie Jones-adjacent tech crossovers.
4. **bh_speed_garage_44** — Speed-garage 4x4 energy at 130: snare backbeat instead of clap, crash on the phrase, tambourine offbeats, rim skips, snare ghost pickup. Reference: Taiki Nulight, DJ Zinc "138 Trek" lineage, bassline/niche.
5. **bh_lean_drop** — Drop discipline over 4 bars: drums stripped to kick/clap/offbeat hat so the bassline leads; hats and rim ghosts creep in per bar, turnaround clap double and kick pickup only in bar 4. Reference: AC Slater & Chris Lorenzo "Fly Kicks" drop sections.
6. **bh_tom_bounce** — Tom/perc bounce cells: melodic low–mid–high tom conversation with conga answers over the kick/clap frame; open hats reduced so toms own the bounce. Reference: My Nu Leng "Masterplan"-style dark rollers.
7. **bh_jackin_uk** — Jackin' clap grammar pulled into UK bass house: mid-velocity claps reinforcing beats 1 and 3 under the loud backbeat, dirty low-perc "jack" on step 15, 0.22 swing, extra 16th open-hat pair in bar 2. Reference: DJ Sneak-derived jackin' recipe via Attack Magazine's Beat Dissected.
8. **bh_night_ride** — Dark warehouse roller: contoured 8th-note ride carries the offbeat energy, snare ghosts smear around the clap, single ghost kick on the "and" of 2 in bar 2. Reference: Taiki Nulight, late-night Night Bass sets.
9. **bh_fill_tom_run** *(fill)* — Groove holds two beats, then a high-to-low tom tumble into an accelerating clap crescendo landing the next downbeat.
10. **bh_fill_garage_snap** *(fill)* — Skippy garage snare fill: rim skips keep the shuffle while syncopated snare snaps build from ghost to two hard swung hits, kick pickup on the final 16th.
