# UK Funky

**Tempo range:** 125–132 BPM (patterns here sit at 126–132; ~130 is home base).

## Drum DNA

UK funky is London's late-2000s collision of funky/tribal house with soca, broken beat, UK garage and grime. The floor is nominally four-on-the-floor, but the defining move is *breaking* it — the kick abandons strict 4x4 for a bar (usually bar 2 or 4) and lands on tresillo-flavoured syncopations before snapping back. On top sits an Afro-Caribbean percussion engine: congas, cowbells, shakers, tambourines and rims phrased in 3+3+2 (tresillo) groupings and off-beat call-and-response, sometimes leaning all the way into the dembow cell. Snares and claps borrow soca's engine-room language — backbeats decorated with off-beat chatter and rolling double-sixteenth pickups. Swing is subtle-to-moderate but the hits stay punchy ("stabbing drums", as Roska's sound is described); percussion lives or dies by its velocity contour, never flat.

## Sound selection & drum machines

UK funky was made fast on Fruity Loops with gathered samples (Roska: early tracks were "straight off of Fruity Loops"), and from about 2012 Roska moved to a Kontakt-centred workflow, pulling soca steel-band percussion from libraries like Laventille Rhythm Section and thickening the low end with a UAD Fatso. The kit is house drums plus a carnival percussion section. Per role:

- **Kick.** A tight, punchy house kick with a short tail (Melodigging: "tight, punchy kick"), with the extra tresillo kicks a few velocity points softer so the break reads as a lurch, not a double. Everything else sidechains to it for the pumping club feel. Owned: Roland TR-909 (default), TR-808 for a rounder afro-swing kick, Korg M1 / Roland MC-303 for the cheap late-2000s preset-kit sound the scene actually used.
- **Snare / clap / rim.** A crisp clap or clap+snare on 2 and 4, and a second tight, dry snare for soca engine-room work — double-16th pickup rolls and offbeat chatter — plus rimshots on the syncopations. Owned: TR-909 clap/snare, TR-808 clap and rim, LinnDrum / Oberheim DMX snares for the broken-beat flavour.
- **Hats.** Syncopated offbeat closed hats and a choked open hat into the bar line, subtly swung with an occasional triplet feel; never a flat 16th carpet — the percussion does that. TR-909 / TR-707 hats (owned).
- **Percussion.** The engine: congas and bongos in call-and-response, cowbell on the 3+3+2, shakers, woodblock/clave, log-drum-style low toms in the afro-swing lane. Owned: Roland TR-727 (congas, bongos, agogo, whistle — the definitive machine for this), TR-808 conga/cowbell/clave, LinnDrum and DMX congas/cabasa/tambourine, Simmons SDS toms. Steel-pan and soca percussion (Laventille Rhythm Section in Kontakt, Roska's own UK Funky sample packs) — get elsewhere.
- **Cymbals.** Crash on phrase starts; rides uncommon.
- **Layering / processing habits.** Leave space — call-and-response phrases with gaps rather than stacked layers; sidechain the percussion bus to the kick; bus compression and Fatso-style saturation for weight. Choose percussion samples with strong natural velocity layers so the contour is audible.
- **Reference tracks.** Roska "Wonderful Day" (2009); Crazy Cousinz "Bongo Jam" (2008); Donae'o "Party Hard" (2009); Lil Silva "Seasons" (2008); Apple "Mr. Bean" (2008); Hard House Banton "Sirens" (2009).

## The 10 approaches

1. **ukfunky_broken_4x4_01** — The signature kick grammar: strict 4x4 in bar 1, tresillo-fronted broken kick (1, 1a, 2a, 3&, 4) in bar 2, clap backbone on 2/4, off-beat hats with an open-hat choke into the bar line. Ref: Roska "Wonderful Day", Crazy Cousinz.
2. **ukfunky_clap_chatter_01** — Soca-derived clap syncopation: snare planted on 2/4 while a clap answers on the 3/6/11/14 sixteenth cell (the off-beat 3+3+2 accents). Ref: Champion, Funkystepz.
3. **ukfunky_soca_engine_01** — Carnival engine room at 132: the kick never breaks (soca keeps the floor), all motion comes from snare double-sixteenth pickup rolls and conga/tambourine layers. Ref: Roska "Squark", Hard House Banton "Sirens".
4. **ukfunky_tribal_conga_01** — Four bars of tribal-house percussion leadership: conga high/low call-and-response evolving every bar, 8th-note tresillo cowbell, kick breaking only in bar 3. Ref: DJ Gregory "Block Party", Karizma, Apple "Mr. Bean".
5. **ukfunky_snare_skank_01** — Snare-led minimalism: stabbing snare accents around a sparse, displaced kick, almost no floor left, barely swung, tension from negative space. Ref: Lil Silva "Night Skanker", "Seasons".
6. **ukfunky_clave_bell_01** — Latin cell construction: full 3-2 son clave as the timeline with an answering cowbell, floor holding in bar 1 and breaking around the clave in bar 2. Ref: Sticky, Geeneus "Yellowtail".
7. **ukfunky_afro_log_01** — The modern afro-swing/amapiano-tinged revival: heavy swing (0.2), sparse displaced kick, low toms as a log-drum-style answer, soft rim instead of a big backbeat, shaker doing the driving. Ref: latter-day Roska, Hagan, Scratcha DVA.
8. **ukfunky_dembow_lean_01** — The dembow strain in funky's DNA: no 2/4 backbeat at all, snare riding the dembow cell over a floor that breaks tresillo-style in bar 2, mid-tom jabs. Ref: Donae'o "Party Hard", Ill Blu.
9. **ukfunky_soca_roll_fill_01** *(fill)* — One-bar accelerating soca snare-line roll, congas doubling, kick dropping out on beat 4 so the returning floor hits harder.
10. **ukfunky_tom_tumble_fill_01** *(fill)* — One-bar high-to-low tom cascade phrased on the tresillo, capped with snare stabs — the tribal percussion-break turnaround.
