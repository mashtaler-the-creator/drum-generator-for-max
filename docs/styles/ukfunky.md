# UK Funky

**Tempo range:** 125–132 BPM (patterns here sit at 126–132; ~130 is home base).

## Drum DNA

UK funky is London's late-2000s collision of funky/tribal house with soca, broken beat, UK garage and grime. The floor is nominally four-on-the-floor, but the defining move is *breaking* it — the kick abandons strict 4x4 for a bar (usually bar 2 or 4) and lands on tresillo-flavoured syncopations before snapping back. On top sits an Afro-Caribbean percussion engine: congas, cowbells, shakers, tambourines and rims phrased in 3+3+2 (tresillo) groupings and off-beat call-and-response, sometimes leaning all the way into the dembow cell. Snares and claps borrow soca's engine-room language — backbeats decorated with off-beat chatter and rolling double-sixteenth pickups. Swing is subtle-to-moderate but the hits stay punchy ("stabbing drums", as Roska's sound is described); percussion lives or dies by its velocity contour, never flat.

## Sound selection & drum machines

UK funky was made fast on Fruity Loops with gathered samples (Roska: early tracks were "straight off of Fruity Loops"), and from about 2012 Roska moved to a Kontakt-centred workflow, pulling soca steel-band percussion from libraries like Laventille Rhythm Section and thickening the low end with a UAD Fatso. The kit is house drums plus a carnival percussion section. Per role:

- **Kick.** A tight, punchy house kick with a short tail (Melodigging: "tight, punchy kick"), with the extra tresillo kicks a few velocity points softer so the break reads as a lurch, not a double. Everything else sidechains to it for the pumping club feel. Owned: Roland TR-909 (default), TR-808 for a rounder afro-swing kick, Korg M1 / Roland MC-303 for the cheap late-2000s preset-kit sound the scene actually used.
- **Snare / clap / rim.** A crisp clap or clap+snare on 2 and 4, and a second tight, dry snare for soca engine-room work — double-16th pickup rolls and offbeat chatter — plus rimshots on the syncopations. Owned: TR-909 clap/snare, TR-808 clap and rim, LinnDrum / Oberheim DMX snares for the broken-beat flavour.
- **Hats.** Syncopated offbeat closed hats and a choked open hat into the bar line, subtly swung with an occasional triplet feel; never a flat 16th carpet — the percussion does that. TR-909 / TR-707 hats.
- **Percussion.** The engine: congas and bongos in call-and-response, cowbell on the 3+3+2, shakers, woodblock/clave, log-drum-style low toms in the afro-swing lane. Owned: Roland TR-727 (congas, bongos, agogo, whistle — the definitive machine for this), TR-808 conga/cowbell/clave, LinnDrum and DMX congas/cabasa/tambourine, Simmons SDS toms. Steel-pan and soca percussion (Laventille Rhythm Section in Kontakt, Roska's own UK Funky sample packs).
- **Cymbals.** Crash on phrase starts; rides uncommon.
- **Layering / processing habits.** Leave space — call-and-response phrases with gaps rather than stacked layers; sidechain the percussion bus to the kick; bus compression and Fatso-style saturation for weight. Choose percussion samples with strong natural velocity layers so the contour is audible.
- **Reference tracks.** Roska "Wonderful Day" (2009); Crazy Cousinz "Bongo Jam" (2008); Donae'o "Party Hard" (2009); Lil Silva "Seasons" (2008); Apple "Mr. Bean" (2008); Hard House Banton "Sirens" (2009).

## The 10 approaches

1. **ukfunky_broken_4x4_01** — The signature kick grammar: strict 4x4 in bar 1, tresillo-fronted broken kick (1, 1a, 2a, 3&, 4) in bar 2, clap backbone on 2/4, off-beat hats with an open-hat choke into the bar line. Ref: Roska "Wonderful Day", Crazy Cousinz.
2. **ukfunky_clap_chatter_01** — Soca-derived clap syncopation: snare planted on 2/4 while a clap answers on the 3/6/11/14 sixteenth cell (the off-beat 3+3+2 accents). Ref: Champion, Funkystepz.
3. **ukfunky_soca_engine_01** — Carnival engine room at 132: the kick never breaks (soca keeps the floor), all motion comes from snare double-sixteenth pickup rolls and conga/tambourine layers. Ref: Roska "Squark", Hard House Banton "Sirens".
4. **ukfunky_tribal_conga_01** — Four bars of tribal-house percussion leadership: conga high/low call-and-response evolving every bar, 8th-note tresillo cowbell, kick breaking only in bar 3. Ref: DJ Gregory "Block Party", Karizma, Apple "Mr. Bean".
5. **ukfunky_snare_skank_01** — Snare-led minimalism: stabbing snare accents around a sparse, displaced kick, almost no floor left, barely swung, tension from negative space. Ref: Lil Silva "Night Skanker", "Seasons".
6. **ukfunky_clave_bell_01** — Latin cell construction: full 3-2 son clave as the timeline with an answering cowbell, floor holding in bar 1 and breaking around the clave in bar 2. Ref: Sticky, Geeneus "Yellowtail".
7. **ukfunky_afro_log_01** — The modern afro-swing/amapiano-tinged revival: heavy swing (0.2), sparse displaced kick, low toms as a log-drum-style answer, soft rim instead of a big backbeat, shaker doing the driving. Ref: latter-day Roska, Hagan, Scratcha DVA.
8. **ukfunky_dembow_lean_01** — The dembow strain in funky's DNA: no 2/4 backbeat at all, snare riding the dembow cell over a floor that breaks tresillo-style in bar 2, mid-tom jabs. Ref: Donae'o "Party Hard", Ill Blu.
9. **ukfunky_grime_stab_01** — The grime/eskibeat strand of the genre's ancestry: cold, spacious rim and snare stabs over a mostly displaced, near-silent kick — negative space and a single hard snare stab per bar instead of a rolling groove, tresillo kick only appearing in bar 2. Ref: Wiley-era eskibeat's frosty, sparse stabbing rhythm language.
10. **ukfunky_broken_beat_skip_01** — The broken-beat (bruk) strand: the kick stays perfectly steady and never breaks, while the snare/rim answer is chopped into frenetic, syncopated broken-beat hits (Bugz in the Attic/IG Culture-style choppy rhythm) with congas and shaker filling the gaps. Ref: broken beat's syncopated, jazz-funk-adjacent rhythm language.

## Fills

- **ukfunky_soca_roll_fill_01** *(fill)* — One-bar accelerating soca snare-line roll, congas doubling, kick dropping out on beat 4 so the returning floor hits harder.
- **ukfunky_tom_tumble_fill_01** *(fill)* — One-bar high-to-low tom cascade phrased on the tresillo, capped with snare stabs — the tribal percussion-break turnaround.

## Risers

- **ukfunky_riser_conga_cascade_01** — Four-bar tribal conga-cascade build: high/low conga call-and-response thickens bar by bar while the tresillo cowbell accelerates from a sparse pulse to a dense 16th cluster; the floor holds 4x4 in bar 1, breaks tresillo-style in bar 2, thins to a single pickup in bar 3, and drops out entirely in bar 4 so a crash plus a conga/tom peak lands on the very last 16th before the drop.
- **ukfunky_riser_clap_roll_drop_01** — Soca clap-roll into the drop on a 32nd grid: bar 1 keeps a lightly broken kick and backbeat clap while a soca snare-line pickup starts late in the bar; bar 2 drops the floor entirely and the snare roll tightens from a swung 16th feel to a full 32nd roll ramping to 127, with the last three 32nds left silent as the gap before the drop.
- **ukfunky_riser_tom_tumble_build_01** — Two-bar escalation of the tribal tom-tumble fill motif: a high-to-low tom cascade phrased on the tresillo thickens each bar while congas double the hits, the floor breaking away from 4x4 into pure tresillo kicks and then dropping its last beat, cowbell driving the tresillo pulse harder in bar 2, crash plus low-tom accent landing together on the final 16th.
- **ukfunky_riser_shaker_lift_01** — Shaker-and-hat density lift into the drop: syncopated offbeat hats and the shaker bed thicken from sparse 8ths to a full swung 16th lattice while the tresillo cowbell accelerates underneath; the kick stays 4x4 in bar 1 and thins to just the downbeat in bar 2 so the open-hat choke and a final crash own the peak.
- **ukfunky_riser_afro_swing_ascent_01** — The afro-swing revival build: the sparse displaced kick and soft rim thin out entirely as low/mid toms answer each other in an ascending log-drum call, shaker driving harder each bar, ending on a rim flourish plus a tomHigh/crash accent that hands off to the drop.

## Breakdowns

- **ukfunky_breakdown_tribal_call_01** — Four-bar percussion-only tribal breakdown: congaHigh/congaLow trade a call-and-response over a cowbell tresillo pulse and shaker bed, tambourine entering in bar 3, no kick at all across all four bars so the returning broken 4x4 floor lands with maximum impact.
- **ukfunky_breakdown_soca_hollow_01** — Soca engine-room hollowed out: the floor drops entirely, leaving only sparse snare pickup ghosts, a light conga/tambourine call and a driving shaker bed to keep the carnival pulse alive until the kick returns for the build.
- **ukfunky_breakdown_clave_space_01** — Minimal Latin-cell space: the son clave timeline is left alone with a light shaker and a ghost cowbell answer, no kick or clap at all, so the timeline itself is the whole groove until the floor and cowbell tresillo return.
- **ukfunky_breakdown_dembow_hollow_01** — The dembow cell hollowed out: the snare rides the dembow rhythm at ghost velocity with no backbeat clap at all, mid-tom jabs answer sparsely, soft rim/shaker bed keeps the pulse until the floor returns tresillo-style.
- **ukfunky_breakdown_carnival_bell_01** — Carnival call-and-response breakdown: cowbell tresillo and tambourine trade across the bar over a low congaLow pulse, a single soft kick opening bar 1 only, everything else at decoration velocity so the returning broken-4x4 floor and clap backbone feel like the carnival arriving.
