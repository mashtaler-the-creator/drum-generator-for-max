# Krushclub (`krushclub`)

**Tempo range:** 140–160 BPM (Melodigging's technical writeup puts the core pocket at 140–165 and notes tracks "alternate between a halftime trap feel and Jersey Club bounce" inside the same song; EDMProd frames it more loosely as TikTok-era EDM built for short, hook-forward clips).

## Drum DNA

Krushclub is a 2023–24 TikTok microgenre, not a decades-old tradition, and its own write-ups agree on the recipe: "a high-energy fusion of Jersey Club rhythms, the distorted, bitcrushed production of HexD, and the pitched-up vocal styles of Hyperpop" (EDMProd, Aesthetics Wiki). Its rhythmic skeleton is borrowed wholesale, not reinvented: the **Jersey Club 5-kick pattern** — kicks on beat 1, beat 2, beat 3, the "a" of 3, and the "&" of 4 of a 16th-note bar — is the same shape Jersey Club has used since the 2000s (the fifth kick lands just before beat 4, giving the "forward pull" that distinguishes it from Baltimore club's triplet feel). RateYourMusic's genre page is explicit that krushclub keeps "either a Jersey Club-based **or** four-on-the-floor rhythm" — some krushclub leans house instead of Jersey, which is why one groove below drops the 5-kick shape entirely.

What actually separates krushclub's kick from plain Jersey Club is everything layered on top and, some of the time, *underneath* it: producers alternate the full Jersey bounce with a **halftime trap feel** — the same track dropping the 5-kick shape for a halftime kick-and-snare-on-3 pocket, clipped 808 glides, and trap-style accelerating hi-hat rolls, before snapping back to the bounce for the hook. RYM also notes krushclub folds in Jerk, Drift Phonk, Funk mandelão and Cumbia — cross-pollinations that show up as gallop kicks, tamborzão-style tom conversations and guacharaca clave ostinatos rather than anything Jersey Club itself does.

On top of the rhythm sits the **HexD layer**: HexD "hexes" a groove by bitcrushing it — reducing bit depth/sample rate for a "crunchy, low-resolution texture reminiscent of old video games" (Aesthetics Wiki) — and krushclub applies that same treatment to its percussion loops and vocal chops, not just the kick. The **hyperpop layer** contributes the pitched-up, heavily-processed vocal chops and a maximalist "more is more" production ethos, plus krushclub's own Sigilkore borrowing: huge, layered clap+snare stacks and a dark, occult-adjacent low end (RYM). Song structure follows TikTok's rules directly — tracks run under two minutes, skip a classic verse/chorus arc, and get to the hook almost immediately (EDMProd).

## Sound selection & drum machines

**Kick.** A layered Jersey Club kick — sub layer, punchier mid layer, optional click layer for small speakers — programmed on the 5-kick shape; some tracks substitute plain four-on-the-floor. Bitcrushed and hard-clipped harder than a plain Jersey Club kick would be. Sample and swing it through an **Akai MPC60/MPC3000** (owned) for period-correct 12-bit crunch, the same workflow UK garage and Jersey Club both used.

**Snare / clap.** A huge layered clap+snare stack on 2 and 4 — both the `snare` and `perc` roles firing together — pitched and distorted for a hyperpop-scale hit, echoing Sigilkore's dark, stacked backbeat. Halftime sections move the single backbeat hit to beat 3 alone. Build from **TR-808** clap/snare layers (owned) plus a pitched/crushed layer from an **Akai S900** (owned) for the lo-fi edge.

**Hats.** Bitcrushed closed 909-style hats (owned: **TR-909**) kept low so the kick shape reads clearly, with trap/hyperpop-derived hi-hat rolls (16ths accelerating into a dense burst) leading into backbeats and hooks — the clearest audible trap/hyperpop fingerprint in the genre.

**Percussion.** The bed-squeak sample (sourced from Trillville's 2004 "Some Cut" intro, per Jersey Club production guides) is krushclub's signature hook element — carried here in the `clave` slot, chopped into the a-of-3/&-of-4 pocket or promoted to a full call-and-response lead. Bitcrushed cowbell/tomLow/perc loops carry the HexD-forward sections; a clipped 808 glide (`tomLow`) stands in for the sub-bass slide. **Elektron Machinedrum** / **Korg Electribe ER-1** (owned) suit the synthesised, crushed percussion loops; **TR-727/TR-505** (owned) congas and claves cover the cumbia/funk-mandelão fusion groove.

**Cymbals.** A single crash marks drop impacts; there is no ride, and cymbal wash plays no role in the genre.

**Layering / processing that drives sample choice.** Bitcrushing (reduced bit depth/sample rate) is applied to percussion loops and vocal chops as a genre-defining effect, not an occasional flourish (Aesthetics Wiki: "the lo-bit quality is the most important part"). Mixing is deliberately "rough around the edges," disregarding conventional compression/limiting wisdom for immediate impact (EDMProd) — pick samples that survive hard clipping and crushing rather than ones with delicate transients.

**Reference tracks.** Lumi Athena — "SMOKE IT OFF!" (2022), "ICEWHORE!" (2023); Odetari — "NARCISSISTIC PERSONALITY DISORDER" (2023); Odetari & Kanii — "Marlboro Man" (2023); Asteria — "WHAT YOU WANT!" (2023); 6arelyhuman — assorted 2023–24 krushclub-tagged flips.

## The 10 approaches

1. **`krushclub_jersey_bounce_01` — Jersey bounce, bitcrushed.** The canonical 5-kick Jersey shape with a chopped bed-squeak (clave) hook and a doubled clap+snare stack. Reference: Lumi Athena "SMOKE IT OFF!".
2. **`krushclub_halftime_trap_flip_01` — Halftime trap flip.** Kick and backbeat move to the halftime trap pocket (kick on 1 + and-of-3, snare on 3), 808 glide underneath, trap hi-hat roll into beat 3. Reference: Odetari / 6arelyhuman-style halftime verses.
3. **`krushclub_bed_squeak_hook_01` — Bed-squeak lead.** The squeak is promoted to the lead hook with a syncopated clave figure answering the 5-kick pattern. Reference: Jersey Club production-guide consensus on the squeak's role.
4. **`krushclub_hexd_bitcrush_loop_01` — HexD percussion lead.** A busy bitcrush-implying perc/cowbell/tomLow loop over a stripped Jersey kick — the percussion loop, not the kick, is the hook. Reference: Aesthetics Wiki's HexD definition.
5. **`krushclub_sigilkore_stack_01` — Sigilkore clap stack.** Huge layered clap+snare on 2 and 4 over a dark low tom pulse; hats reduced to ghosts. Reference: RYM's Sigilkore cross-pollination note.
6. **`krushclub_gallop_jerk_01` — Gallop/Jerk kick.** A soft gallop kick before 2 and 4 skips the low end like a Jerk beat, rim ghosts fill the pocket. Reference: RYM's Jerk fusion note.
7. **`krushclub_triplet_grid_01` — Triplet-feel Jersey.** The 5-kick shape at swing 0.33 to approximate the triplet/tresillo pull some Jersey Club and krushclub flips lean into. Reference: BeatsToRapOn / Orphiq Jersey Club guides.
8. **`krushclub_cumbia_mandelao_01` — Cumbia / funk mandelão fusion.** Four-on-the-floor kick, guacharaca-style clave ostinato, tamborzão-style conga call-and-response. Reference: RYM's Cumbia/Funk mandelão note.
9. **`krushclub_hyperpop_hat_roll_01` — Hyperpop hat roll.** A trap/hyperpop hi-hat roll accelerates into every backbeat hit over the Jersey kick, the loudest decoration in the mix. Reference: EDMProd / Aesthetics Wiki hyperpop layer.
10. **`krushclub_four_floor_warehouse_01` — Four-on-the-floor branch.** Plain house kick with offbeat open hat and a bitcrushed cowbell/perc loop — proof the fusion works without the Jersey shape. Reference: RYM's "four-on-the-floor" alternative.

Extra one-bar fill (tag `fill`, auto-injected every 4th bar): **`krushclub_fill_squeak_stutter_01`** — the 5-kick shape holds through beat 3, then the bed squeak and clap stutter into a 16th run through beat 4 as the kick drops out.

## Risers

1. **`krushclub_riser_hat_roll_01`** — Trap/hyperpop hi-hat-roll build: hats climb from spaced 16ths to a dense wall over two bars while the kick thins to the downbeat; crash on the last 16th.
2. **`krushclub_riser_gallop_build_01`** — The gallop kick intensifies into a full 16th kick roll ramping to 127, then cuts to silence on the very last 16th.
3. **`krushclub_riser_halftime_double_01`** — Halftime-to-doubletime snare build: the beat-3-only halftime backbeat accelerates through 8ths into a full 16th roll as the kick disappears.
4. **`krushclub_riser_squeak_climb_01`** — The bed-squeak hook itself climbs in density and velocity instead of the kick, joined by a rising cowbell layer.
5. **`krushclub_riser_silence_drop_01`** — Stutter-stop build: hats and claps build for a bar and a half, then hard silence for three 16ths before one final kick+clap+crash hit.

## Breakdowns

1. **`krushclub_breakdown_squeak_only_01`** — Kick and clap removed; only the bed-squeak call-and-response and a soft shaker carry the section.
2. **`krushclub_breakdown_halftime_kick_01`** — Kick on beat 1 only, muted 808 glide, single soft clap on beat 3 — the halftime "verse" pocket.
3. **`krushclub_breakdown_perc_bed_01`** — The HexD percussion loop alone at decoration velocity, no kick; one snare on beat 4 of bar 2 cues the return.
4. **`krushclub_breakdown_ghost_hats_01`** — No kick or clap; ghost-velocity hats and a lone rim hold the grid open for a vocal chop to sit alone.
5. **`krushclub_breakdown_808_glide_01`** — The clipped 808 glide alone under sparse squeak accents; one pickup kick on the downbeat of bar 2.

## Swing mapping

Krushclub inherits Jersey Club's grid, and Jersey Club is normally programmed on straight 16ths even though it's often *described* as triplet/tresillo-feeling because of the fifth kick's near-upbeat placement — so most patterns here use `swing: 0`. Where a flip leans into the looser triplet pull explicitly, `swing: 0.33` approximates an 8th-note triplet subdivision on the engine's straight-16th grid (the `krushclub_triplet_grid_01` groove). There is no MPC-swing-percentage convention specific to krushclub in the sources; treat 0–0.08 as "tight/straight" and 0.33 as the triplet-feel outlier.
