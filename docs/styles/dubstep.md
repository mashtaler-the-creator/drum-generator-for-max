# Dubstep / UK 140

**Tempo range:** 138–142 BPM (the kick/snare skeleton feels like ~70 BPM half-time).

## Drum DNA

Dubstep drums are built on the halfstep frame: kick on beat 1, a single snare or rim shot on beat 3 — never on 2 and 4. The space between those anchors is the actual instrument; syncopated kicks creep in toward the end of the bar with a light-to-heavy 16th swing inherited from UK garage, and the mid-frequency gap is filled (or deliberately left empty) by rims, shakers, tambourines and congas rather than busy hats. Hats and shakers typically pause on beat 3 to let the snare breathe, and odd-grid (32nd/triplet-feel) details are kept toward the end of the bar.

## Sound selection & drum machines

The DMZ-era kit is small and heavy: 808 and 707 family hits, a few acoustic percussion samples, and dub-style echo/reverb sends doing most of the work. Mala programs in NI Battery (importing his own samples) after a decade in Reason's Redrum; Loefah's rule is "space is just as much an instrument as a kick or a snare". Per role:

- **Kick.** A raw, hollow kick with deep low end — Attack's Mystik dissection points at Coki's "Goblin" as the model — or a TR-808 kick overdriven and sent to echo/reverb, played monophonically (sampler set to one voice) so tails never overlap (Studio Brootle). Owned: Roland TR-808 (the anchor), TR-909 for the wobble-era stomp, Akai MPC60 / S900 for 12-bit weight, Jomox Xbase09 for a tunable analogue 808-style kick.
- **Snare / clap / rim.** Two schools. Meditative: a layered electronic snare with body around 160–200 Hz plus a high-passed reverb clap to fill the top, or a single acoustic snare through filter-delay, compression and overdrive for grit (Attack). Minimal: a TR-707 or TR-808 snare or just a rim shot carrying beat 3 (Loefah). TR-909 clap under the snare in the stomp lane; rims from 808 / 707 / LinnDrum for dub-delayed answers.
- **Hats.** Sparse. Short gated maracas or closed hats (sampler envelope shortened) punctuating the space, a mix of acoustic and drum-machine hits, typically pausing on beat 3 so the snare breathes; open hats only in the Coki/Rusko stomp. TR-808 / TR-707 hats, Korg Minipops / CR-78 for soft maraca-like ticks.
- **Percussion.** Acoustic shaker with a breathing pattern, tambourine as two samples at slightly different pitches, cajón/congas high-passed around 100 Hz, and stray congas in the Commodo lane (acoustic percussion libraries, or TR-727 congas as a stand-in). Dub-delayed rim or snare "delay tails" as percussion.
- **Cymbals.** Almost none; reverse crashes, dub sirens and reverb swells mark sections instead.
- **Layering / processing habits.** Everything routes to echo and reverb sends with low-cut filters on the delay so tails stay clean; a drum-buss drive glues the kit. Choose samples with weight and a clean decay — they will be heard mostly through their echoes.
- **Reference tracks.** Digital Mystikz "Anti War Dub" (2006); Loefah "Horror Show" (2005); Skream "Midnight Request Line" (2005); Coki "Spongebob" (2007); Mala "Alicia" (2007); Kahn & Neek "Percy" (2013).

## The 10 approaches

1. **`dubstep_dmz_meditative_halfstep`** — 4-bar DMZ-era meditative halfstep: one anchor kick per bar, late swung kick every other bar, breathing shaker that drops out on beat 3, sparse rim answers. Ref: Mala "Changes", Digital Mystikz "Anti War Dub".
2. **`dubstep_loefah_808_space`** — stripped 808 minimalism where a rim shot carries beat 3 instead of a snare and almost nothing else happens; negative space as an instrument. Ref: Loefah "Horror Show", "Mud".
3. **`dubstep_horsepower_shuffle`** — early garage-descended dark 2-step: heavy 0.25 swing, skippy velocity-laddered hats, paired rim shots, ghost snares around the backbone. Ref: Horsepower Productions "Fist of Fury", El-B "Buck & Bury".
4. **`dubstep_coki_wobble_stomp`** — wobble-era stomp: near-straight grid, kick on 1 plus the "& of 3", clap layered under the snare, driving 8th hats and open-hat lifts. Ref: Coki "Spongebob", Rusko-era tearout.
5. **`dubstep_commodo_swagger`** — modern Deep Medi hip-hop-inflected 140: lazily swung (0.22) late kicks pushed against beat 3, loose tambourine, rim doubles, stray congas. Ref: Commodo "Shift", Commodo/Gantz/Kahn "Volume One".
6. **`dubstep_bandulu_gunfinger`** — Bristol grime-140: the Pulse X kick cell (1, a-of-1, & of 3) as structural backbone under snare+clap, everything else nearly empty. Ref: Kahn & Neek "Percy", Boofy & Kahn "Cut Sound".
7. **`dubstep_mystik_percussion`** — percussion-layered meditative construction from Attack Magazine's "Mystik Dubstep" dissection: 16th shaker pausing on beat 3, two-velocity tambourine, congas, soft final kick, one dub-delay rim per two bars. Ref: Mala "Alicia", Truth-style deep rollers.
8. **`dubstep_dungeon_glitch_32`** — modern dungeon-140 using a 32-step grid for 32nd rim ruffs and hat stutters kept toward the end of the bar; sparse halfstep frame elsewhere. Ref: Commodo "Kibosh"-era glitch feel, Hi5Ghost/Boofy oddness.
9. **`dubstep_riddim_robotic_01`** — modern riddim-style: a deliberate break from "never on 2 and 4" — kicks on 1 and 3 plus 2 and 4 for a stiff, robotic, near-4/4 stomp with almost no swing. Ref: EDMProd "How to Make Riddim like Subtronics", Ternion Sound-style riddim.
10. **`dubstep_triplet_swing_shuffle_01`** — true triplet-grid construction: the halfstep frame stays intact under the library's heaviest swing (0.25) plus a conga cross-rhythm landing every third 16th step. Ref: Studio Brootle "Dubstep Drum Patterns" (triplet-grid tips).

## Fills

1. **`dubstep_crossover_rush_fill`** (1 bar) — Hamdi-era crossover energy: double-speed hat rush into a rising snare build with tom accents. Ref: Hamdi "Skanka" transitions.
2. **`dubstep_dub_space_fill`** (1 bar) — dub-wise sparse fill: a decaying rim "delay tail" after the snare plus one low tom pickup; for the meditative material. Ref: DMZ dub-sirens-and-space transitions.

## Risers

1. **`dubstep_riser_subdrop_silence_01`** — halfstep bar 1 into a bar 2 with the kick dropped, a tom cascade stacking into an accelerating snare/perc roll, then total silence for the last two 16ths — the "strategic silence right before the drop." Ref: KAN Samples dubstep arrangement guide; Point Blank dubstep drop guide.
2. **`dubstep_riser_halfstep_to_double_01`** — true 8ths→16ths→32nds acceleration on a 32nd grid: the kick drops, the snare doubles all the way to a 32nd roll, and the last two 32nds sit silent. Ref: MusicRadar snare-roll build-up guide; Attack Magazine "10 Snare Rolls For The Drop".
3. **`dubstep_riser_reload_rewind_01`** — the reload moment itself: a crash-marked bar 1 hit, then a continuous snare/perc roll in bar 2 climbing from mid-velocity to 127 across all 16 steps — the pre-reload intensity that gets a tune wheeled up. Ref: DJ TechTools "Reload: A History of The Rewind".
4. **`dubstep_riser_wobble_lfo_ramp_01`** — a perc pulse tracks the "LFO rate from quarters to sixteenths" wobble-automation trick while the kick thins to a single downbeat and a tom stab punctuates the reveal. Ref: 4D4M "How to Create the Perfect Dubstep Drop".
5. **`dubstep_riser_mc_hype_stab_01`** — off-beat perc/cowbell stabs in the final bar stand in for the processed "drop"/"fire"/"selecta" vocal chops sound-system culture places before a drop, climaxing on a crash. Ref: Point Blank "How to Create a Dubstep Drop from Scratch".

## Breakdowns

1. **`dubstep_breakdown_subonly_drift_01`** — sparse-style breakdown: drums drop out almost entirely so only a soft kick every other bar and a slow-breathing shaker remain, per the "sub bass alone" arrangement pattern. Ref: KAN Samples dubstep arrangement guide.
2. **`dubstep_breakdown_halftime_atmosphere_01`** — dense-style breakdown: kick/snare thin to near silence while shaker, tambourine and congas keep atmospheric energy alive under the halftime frame. Ref: KAN Samples dubstep arrangement guide.
3. **`dubstep_breakdown_dub_echo_tail_01`** — negative-space minimalism built from implied dub-echo tails: a single rim shot per bar decays through descending "repeat" velocities, no kick or snare at all. Ref: Junkee "Loefah: Lower than Low".
4. **`dubstep_breakdown_congas_meditative_01`** — four-bar Mala-school meditative breakdown: congas and tambourine trade soft phrases, kick silent until a single low downbeat announces bar 4. Ref: XLR8R "Building an Iconic Sound: Mala".
5. **`dubstep_breakdown_808_space_hollow_01`** — the most extreme negative-space breakdown in the kit: a rim shot alone carries beat 3, everything else silent, Loefah-school hollow minimalism. Ref: Junkee "Loefah: Lower than Low".
