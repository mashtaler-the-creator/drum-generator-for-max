# Drum & Bass (style: `dnb`)

**Tempo range:** ~170–176 BPM (halftime patterns keep the 170 grid but feel like 85).

## Drum DNA

Drum & bass drums are built on the **two-step skeleton**: kick on beat 1 plus one syncopated kick (most often the "and" of beat 3, or the "and" of beat 2 in jump-up), with snares locked hard on beats 2 and 4 — the one law almost every subgenre obeys. The exception is halftime/autonomic, which drops the snare to beat 3 only. Between the backbeats lives the **ghost-snare micro-language**: quiet snare hits at the "e" and "a" positions (especially the 2.5 and 4.5 offbeat spots) that give the groove its roll without cluttering it — direct descendants of jungle's chopped-break ghost chatter. The top end defines the subgenre as much as the kick pattern: ride-driven 8ths for deep rollers, swung open hats and shakers for liquid, on-beat hats choking an offbeat open-hat skank for jump-up, surgically micro-edited 32nd hat stutters for neurofunk, and relentless accented 16ths for dancefloor. Kick and backbeat snare are always the loudest events; everything else lives well below them.

## Sound selection & drum machines

**Break heritage.** DnB never fully left jungle's chopped-break vocabulary behind, even once its kicks and snares became mostly programmed. **Amen** (The Winstons) ghost-snare chatter still supplies the "raw"/sample-based lineage's texture (early Bad Company, Source Direct, Kemistry & Storm); **Think** and **Apache**-style congas colour the liquid palette; **Funky Drummer** and **Tighten Up** surface in halftime/autonomic's skeletal, off-kilter syncopation. All are owned via the 10 Most Iconic Amen Patterns pack, the junglebreaks.co.uk collection, Neekeetone Jungle Breaks and an assorted funk/soul break library, chopped and timestretched on the same **Akai S900/S950/S1000** lineage documented for jungle.

**Kick.** A punchy, sub-reinforced **TR-909/808**-style kick (owned) for the two-step skeleton, layered under or alongside a chopped break kick where the production leans "raw"; foghorn/jump-up producers now typically design the main kick from scratch (Sonic Academy Kick 2, get elsewhere) so it doesn't phase against other DJs' kicks in a mix.

**Snare / clap / rim.** A composite 909 snare/clap stack (owned) carries the 2-and-4 law, doubled with a sampled break snare (Amen/Think) for grit in rollers and liquid; halftime uses a single glassy snare on beat 3 with rim ticks standing in for the missing backbeat.

**Hats.** 909 ride 8ths (owned) drive rollers; swung, occasionally open hats carry liquid; tight choked hats plus an offbeat open-hat skank define jump-up; Elektron Machinedrum-style (owned) mechanically edited hat stutters at 32nd resolution are neurofunk's signature.

**Percussion.** Conga/bongo hits (TR-727/505, owned) colour liquid and jump-up; halftime/autonomic favours glassy rim ticks and clave pings; neurofunk substitutes filtered, edited percussion loops for anything organic.

**Cymbals.** A single crash marks phrase starts in dancefloor/jump-up anthems; the ride otherwise does all the cymbal-adjacent groove work.

**Layering / processing that affects sample choice.** Keep the ghost-snare layer's low end cut below ~200 Hz so it never competes with the backbeat snare or the kick; EQ and multiband-compress chopped break material the same way jungle does before blending it with programmed 909/808 layers; neurofunk's tension comes from micro-editing density, not from adding more layers.

**Reference tracks.** Alix Perez — Everyday Something (2011); Calibre — Musch (2004); Noisia — Machine Gun (2010); Bou — Turbulence (2020); dBridge & Instra:mental — Chameleon (2009); Ed Rush & Optical — Wormhole (1998).

## The 10 approaches

1. **`dnb_rolling_01`** — *neutral two-step*. The plain baseline: kick on 1, snare locked to 2 and 4, a rolling closed-hat 16th pattern and a single ride marker — the skeleton the other nine approaches vary from.
2. **`dnb_deep_roller_01`** — *rolling / deep*. Ride-driven roller: strict two-step (kick 1 + and-of-3), 8th-note ride carrying the momentum, 16th hat shading on the off-16ths, and ghost snares that migrate position every bar across the 4-bar phrase. Refs: Alix Perez, Skeptical, Halogenix ("Independent").
3. **`dnb_liquid_flow_01`** — *liquid*. Smooth two-step with heavy swing (0.12), ghost snares at the 2.5/4.5 positions, flowing 16th shaker, offbeat hats with occasional open hats, soft rim and conga colour. Refs: Calibre, Lenzman, LSB.
4. **`dnb_jumpup_bounce_01`** — *jump-up (classic lineage)*. Kicks on 1 and the and-of-2, snares slammed with a clap layer, on-beat closed hats choking an offbeat open-hat skank, near-zero ghosts — direct and bouncy. Refs: DJ Hazard lineage into Bou, Hedex.
5. **`dnb_neuro_engine_01`** — *neurofunk*. 32 steps/bar for micro-edits: simple dry kick/snare placement, but 32nd ghost-snare chatter, stuttered hat articulations and a ramping edit into the loop point. Tension from the edits, not the skeleton. Refs: Noisia, Mefjus.
6. **`dnb_dancefloor_drive_01`** — *dancefloor*. Crash-marked downbeat, accented driving 16th hats, offbeat tambourine, clap-layered backbeats, bar-4 open-hat/snare-pickup push into the next phrase. Refs: Sub Focus, Dimension, Culture Shock.
7. **`dnb_halftime_ghost_01`** — *minimal / autonomic 85-170 halftime*. Snare on beat 3 only, skeletal kicks that shift per bar, glassy rim ticks, clave pings, broken non-continuous hats and soft shaker 8ths — negative space as the instrument. Refs: dBridge, Instra:mental (Autonomic).
8. **`dnb_techstep_march_01`** — *techstep heritage*. Cold unswung march: hard two-step, mechanical near-flat 16th hats, quarter-note ride ticks, one industrial low-tom stab, almost no ghost warmth. Refs: Ed Rush & Optical, early Bad Company.
9. **`dnb_foghorn_stomp_01`** — *foghorn-era modern jump-up*. Drums stripped to maximum weight at 176: kick 1 + and-of-3, huge clap-layered snares, whisper-level offbeat hats, dancehall conga bounce — all midrange left free for the foghorn bass. Refs: Hedex, Bou drop sections.
10. **`dnb_amen_hybrid_roller_01`** — *amen-hybrid / raw*. A chopped-amen ghost pattern (16th/32nd chatter) layered under a strict programmed two-step kick/snare and an 8th ride — the sample-based lineage that never let go of the break. Refs: early Bad Company, Source Direct, Kemistry & Storm.

Two one-bar fills (tag `fill`, auto-injected every 4th bar): **`dnb_fill_snare_roll_01`** (dancefloor: groove holds through beat 2, then a 16th snare roll ramps from ghost-soft to full slam across the back half), **`dnb_fill_ghost_break_01`** (roller/liquid: amen-flavoured turnaround with ghost chatter, a displaced snare and a tom cascade as the ride fades).

## Risers

1. **`dnb_riser_snare_roll_16bar_01`** — The classic 16-bar build compressed to three bars: kick thins to beat 1 then drops entirely as the snare roll accelerates from 8ths to an unbroken 16th roll ramping 60→127, hats thinning to nothing.
2. **`dnb_riser_second_drop_reset_01`** — The "reset" build out of a breakdown into a harder second drop: sparse kick+rim, then a building 8th ride and hats, then a doubled-16th hat pattern and a snare pickup roll into a crash-topped final hit.
3. **`dnb_riser_neuro_edit_stutter_01`** — Neurofunk micro-edit stutter: the dry, mechanical neuro-engine skeleton stays put while snare/hat stutter-edits ramp tension at 32nd resolution, then cut completely silent for the last four steps — the glitch-cut before the drop.
4. **`dnb_riser_jumpup_hype_01`** — Jump-up hype build: the punchy bounce intensifies rather than stripping back, the offbeat open-hat skank doubles to 16ths, and a clap roll fires into a crash-topped final hit.
5. **`dnb_riser_halftime_lift_01`** — Halftime-to-fulltime lift: bar 1 sits in halftime (snare on beat 3 only), bar 2 quietly reintroduces 2-and-4, and bar 3 commits to full two-step with an accelerating snare roll into the drop.

## Breakdowns

1. **`dnb_breakdown_drumless_atmosphere_01`** — Drumless atmosphere: almost nothing plays beyond a rim tick and a whisper of shaker per bar, the "emotional reset" that lets pads/vocals/sub breathe.
2. **`dnb_breakdown_halftime_pad_01`** — Pad section: only ride and hats carry a halftime pulse — no kick, no snare — so a held pad or vocal owns the space.
3. **`dnb_breakdown_neuro_tension_01`** — Neuro tension: no kick at all, a mechanical rim/hat tick pattern decaying in velocity bar to bar as if trailing into a long reverb tail before the drum fill returns.
4. **`dnb_breakdown_liquid_rideshaker_01`** — Liquid breakdown: no kick, just a soft ride-and-shaker wash with the odd conga colour hit, keeping the Calibre/Lenzman warmth going.
5. **`dnb_breakdown_subonly_kick_01`** — Sub-only reset: one soft kick pulse per bar standing in for the sub anchor, a rim ghost and a whisper of shaker — the quietest breakdown here, built to make the next build's snare roll feel enormous by contrast.
