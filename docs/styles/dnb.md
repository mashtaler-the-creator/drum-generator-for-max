# Drum & Bass (style: `dnb`)

**Tempo range:** ~170–176 BPM (halftime patterns keep the 170 grid but feel like 85).

## Drum DNA

Drum & bass drums are built on the **two-step skeleton**: kick on beat 1 plus one syncopated kick (most often the "and" of beat 3, or the "and" of beat 2 in jump-up), with snares locked hard on beats 2 and 4 — the one law almost every subgenre obeys. The exception is halftime/autonomic, which drops the snare to beat 3 only. Between the backbeats lives the **ghost-snare micro-language**: quiet snare hits at the "e" and "a" positions (especially the 2.5 and 4.5 offbeat spots) that give the groove its roll without cluttering it. The top end defines the subgenre as much as the kick pattern: ride-driven 8ths for deep rollers, swung open hats and shakers for liquid, on-beat hats choking an offbeat open-hat skank for jump-up, surgically micro-edited 32nd hat stutters for neurofunk, and relentless accented 16ths for dancefloor. Kick and backbeat snare are always the loudest events; everything else lives well below them.

## The 10 approaches

1. **`dnb_deep_roller_01`** — *rolling / deep*. Ride-driven roller: strict two-step (kick 1 + and-of-3), 8th-note ride carrying the momentum, 16th hat shading on the off-16ths, and ghost snares that migrate position every bar across the 4-bar phrase. Refs: Alix Perez, Skeptical, Halogenix ("Independent").
2. **`dnb_liquid_flow_01`** — *liquid*. Smooth two-step with heavy swing (0.12), ghost snares at the 2.5/4.5 positions, flowing 16th shaker, offbeat hats with occasional open hats, soft rim and conga colour. Refs: Calibre, Lenzman, LSB.
3. **`dnb_jumpup_bounce_01`** — *jump-up (classic lineage)*. Kicks on 1 and the and-of-2, snares slammed with a clap layer, on-beat closed hats choking an offbeat open-hat skank, near-zero ghosts — direct and bouncy. Refs: DJ Hazard lineage into Bou, Hedex.
4. **`dnb_neuro_engine_01`** — *neurofunk*. 32 steps/bar for micro-edits: simple dry kick/snare placement, but 32nd ghost-snare chatter, stuttered hat articulations and a ramping edit into the loop point. Tension from the edits, not the skeleton. Refs: Noisia, Mefjus.
5. **`dnb_dancefloor_drive_01`** — *dancefloor*. Crash-marked downbeat, accented driving 16th hats, offbeat tambourine, clap-layered backbeats, bar-4 open-hat/snare-pickup push into the next phrase. Refs: Sub Focus, Dimension, Culture Shock.
6. **`dnb_halftime_ghost_01`** — *minimal / autonomic 85-170 halftime*. Snare on beat 3 only, skeletal kicks that shift per bar, glassy rim ticks, clave pings, broken non-continuous hats and soft shaker 8ths — negative space as the instrument. Refs: dBridge, Instra:mental (Autonomic).
7. **`dnb_techstep_march_01`** — *techstep heritage*. Cold unswung march: hard two-step, mechanical near-flat 16th hats, quarter-note ride ticks, one industrial low-tom stab, almost no ghost warmth. Refs: Ed Rush & Optical, early Bad Company.
8. **`dnb_foghorn_stomp_01`** — *foghorn-era modern jump-up*. Drums stripped to maximum weight at 176: kick 1 + and-of-3, huge clap-layered snares, whisper-level offbeat hats, dancehall conga bounce — all midrange left free for the foghorn bass. Refs: Hedex, Bou drop sections.
9. **`dnb_fill_snare_roll_01`** — *fill, dancefloor*. Groove holds through beat 2, then a 16th snare roll ramps from ghost-soft (48) to full slam (108) across the back half — the classic phrase-end lift.
10. **`dnb_fill_ghost_break_01`** — *fill, roller/liquid*. Amen-flavoured turnaround: ghost chatter around the backbeats, displaced mid-velocity snare on the and-of-3, high→mid→low tom cascade on the last three 16ths as the ride fades.
